# Data and software attribution

- `data/reference/Coordinates.csv`: a 204-location selection adapted from the Simplemaps World Cities Basic database v1.901 (https://simplemaps.com/data/world-cities), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Location labels were harmonized to the study labels; 202 selections use the largest recorded city population. US Virgin Islands uses the first listed Charlotte Amalie record because populations were missing; Tokelau is the archived manually supplied Atafu point (-8.54212, -172.51591). Coordinates are representative points, not centroids or national exposures. The full Simplemaps database is not included.
- `data/reference/geometry_reference_points.csv`: points derived from Natural Earth Admin 0 Countries 10m v5.1.1 (203 locations) and Natural Earth 50m tiny countries (Tokelau). Natural Earth data are public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/. The largest valid polygon was projected to a country-centred Lambert azimuthal equal-area CRS, `st_point_on_surface` applied, then returned to EPSG:4326. Source-label coordinates defined the projection centre. All 203 derived points were checked against their source geometry.
- GBD/IHME input estimates and SDI are not redistributed by this repository. Access and publication uses remain governed by IHME: https://www.healthdata.org/data-tools-practices/data-access .
- World Bank WDI PM2.5 input: indicator EN.ATM.PM25.MC.M3, micrograms per cubic metre. See the relevant World Bank data terms and indicator metadata: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EN.ATM.PM25.MC.M3 . The frozen historical matrix is excluded; current API values can differ from that snapshot.
- R/Python dependencies retain their respective licenses. No dependency source is bundled.
