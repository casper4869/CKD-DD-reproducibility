# Fixed age-reference sensitivity contract

Editor request E.3 concerns shared age dependence of CKD and depressive-disorder incidence. This is one descriptive sensitivity of the 2021 within-country, within-sex correlation across 19 five-year age groups from 5–9 through 95+ years. The existing original-rate analysis and all other scientific results remain fixed.

The two existing immutable GBD 2021 CSV extracts contain 204 countries and territories and no official Global aggregate. Therefore the reference is explicitly an equal-country-weighted cross-country age profile, not a population-weighted or official GBD global rate. For each target country, sex and disease, average the age-specific rates of the other 203 countries. Regress the target country's 19 rates on its disease-specific reference profile plus an intercept, separately for CKD and depressive disorders. The Pearson correlation of the two residual vectors is the sole reference-adjusted estimand. The slope accommodates a country's rate scale, while the reference profile removes the shared cross-country age shape. Leave-one-country-out construction avoids including the target country in its own reference.

No alternative transformations, choice of age groups, country exclusions, reference weighting, significance thresholds, or statistical models will be selected after observing the result. Report every country and both sexes, the original correlation, adjusted correlation, reference-explained variance for each disease, and all 19 reference values and residual pairs. No per-country hypothesis tests are planned, because adjacent age-specific modelled rates are dependent. Positive signs are descriptive and are not evidence of individual-level comorbidity or prediction.

The deterministic quality target is complete and correct response to the editor: 408 unaltered original correlations replicated; 408 adjusted profiles with 19 observations each; exact orthogonality of fitted residuals to intercept/reference within numerical tolerance; complete auditable results with immutable input SHA256 hashes and runnable code. No favorable sign or P value is a success metric. Countries are the unit for descriptive paired bootstrap intervals (2,000 replicates, fixed seed 20261115) for medians and the median paired correlation difference. These intervals reflect country heterogeneity only, not GBD uncertainty draws or independent age measurements.

One analytical iteration is allowed. An independent checker should verify the calculations and claim calibration before acceptance. Possible scientific outcome: adjustment may attenuate, reverse or retain positive correlations. All outcomes will be reported identically; no rerun with a new specification will be used to seek a positive result.

Inputs: `CKD_age_2021.csv`, `DD_age_2021.csv` from the existing v3 immutable input snapshot; verification comparator `S6_age_profile_descriptive.csv` is read-only and not used to fit any model. This contract is written before executing the reference-adjusted calculations.
